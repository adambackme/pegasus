How long until I see results?
+
Google rankings typically start moving within 30 days of starting SEO. Most clients report a measurable increase in calls between 45–90 days. For Google Ads, calls can start within 48 hours of campaign launch. You'll get a monthly report with exact position data — no guessing.
Is there a contract?
+
No contracts. Website projects are 50% upfront, 50% on delivery — you own it outright. SEO and Ads retainers are month-to-month, cancel anytime. We keep clients because of results, not lock-in clauses.
How does payment work?
+
We invoice via PayPal Business — secure, worldwide, and covered by PayPal's buyer protection. Pay by card or PayPal balance. Proper invoice for every payment. No hidden fees, no surprises.
Do I need to get on a call?
+
Not at all. Everything — audit, proposal, onboarding, revisions, monthly reports — happens by email. Faster and cleaner for everyone. If you'd prefer a call at any point, happy to arrange one. But most clients never need it.
What if my website is already decent?
+
Then you may only need the SEO retainer. The free audit will tell us clearly. We won't recommend a new site if you don't need one — honest advice earns better clients than an oversell.
What industries do you work with?
+
Any local service business: dentists, chiropractors, lawyers, plumbers, electricians, roofers, HVAC, auto repair, restaurants, salons, vets, optometrists, physio, contractors. If your business depends on local customers finding you, we can help.
Do you work outside the US?
+
Yes — actively serving the UK, Germany, Netherlands, France, and wider Europe. Local SEO works the same everywhere: your business needs to show up when people in your area search. Geography doesn't change the fundamentals.
What's in the free audit?
+
Your Google ranking for your main keywords, mobile/desktop speed score, breakdown of what your top 3 competitors are doing differently, and a written action plan with 3–5 prioritised changes. Delivered within 24 hours. No sales pitch embedded — just data.
